Week 2 · August 31 – September 4

Java Review & Designing Position

Take one concept from the game — a square on the board — and model it properly in Java, end to end. Monday we design it; Wednesday we write it.

Milestone due: M0b — Wednesday, September 9, 11:59 PM

📋Topics covered

🌞 🌙
  • Choosing a construct — class vs. record vs. enum: identity, value, closed set.
  • Equality — == vs. equals, why hashCode travels with it, and what breaks in sets and maps without them.
  • Records — generated equality, immutability, and why Position is one.
  • Enums with behaviour — Color, and putting the answer where the data is.
  • Validation — compact constructors and making invalid positions impossible.
  • Throw vs. empty answer — why the constructor throws and offsetOrNull returns null.
  • The coordinate convention — two 0-based ints, file first, and the alternatives it beat.

  4. Submit by tag. git tag submit-m0b && git push origin main --tags — then verify the tag shows on GitHub.
