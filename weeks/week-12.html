Week 12 · November 9 – 13

Debugging

One meeting this week: how to find a bug on purpose, with stack traces, the IntelliJ debugger, and logging, and how to read code you did not write. There is no class Wednesday (Veterans Day).

Due: M6 — Monday, November 9, 11:59 PM · Opens: M7 — Monday, November 9

📋Topics covered

🌞 🌙
Bring a laptop with IntelliJ on Monday if you can. We debug a small program together.

Monday — Debugging

  • A method: reproduce, read the evidence, guess, test the guess, fix and keep a test.
  • Stack traces: the parts, which frame to open, Caused by, and what was null.
  • The IntelliJ debugger: breakpoints, stepping, evaluating expressions, conditional breakpoints, and the call stack.
  • Logging with java.util.logging: levels, configuration, and which tool to reach for.
  • Reading code you did not write, and three failures in a generated Swing view.
