3. Coupling: who knows about whom

Coupling is how much one part of the program must know about another to do its job. Measured by one question: if this changes, what breaks?

Aren't the pieces' moves "rules"? Why model?

A piece's geometry is a fact about the piece, like its colour. You could write Knight correctly for an infinite empty board.

What a piece cannot know: anything about the rest of the board. Whose turn. Whether its own king is safe.

Facts about one piece: model. Facts about the whole position: engine. That is why engine opened the week you needed "every move for White".
